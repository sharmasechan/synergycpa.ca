2025 CRA Corporate Tax Filing Reminders

Jan 2025 · 3 min read

Key deadlines, slips, and tips to make year-end painless—what to prep and how to avoid penalties.

Deadlines to remember

  • T2 return: due 6 months after year-end.
  • Balance owing: due 2 months after year-end (3 months for some CCPCs).
  • Instalments: monthly or quarterly—track changes in taxable income.

What to prepare

  • Trial balance, bank recs, AR/AP aging, fixed asset updates.
  • HST/GST filings aligned with books.
  • T4/T5 and shareholder loan schedules if applicable.

Avoid common pitfalls

  • Unreconciled bank/credit card accounts.
  • Unrecorded accruals, payroll liabilities, or WIP.
  • Missing source docs—adopt a receipts workflow.
